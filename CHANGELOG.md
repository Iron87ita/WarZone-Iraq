Initial WarZone Iraq repository publication, using the supplied `WarZone_IRAQ_IRON87_V1.0_NORTH_BLUE_SOUTH_RED.miz` unchanged.

- Northern BLUE and southern RED deployment across six progressive sectors and 42 land objectives.
- WarZone Caucasus 4.48 campaign logic adapted to Iraq, including F10 AI controls, live opposing AI tasks and logistics.

- Author-supplied campaign banner, English and Italian overview, installation instructions, F10 guide, sector list and package checksums.

The public release version is 1.0. Internal Lua and save identifiers retain V448 for compatibility. Publication does not introduce additional gameplay changes or claim an in-simulator test pass.
